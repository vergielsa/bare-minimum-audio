About Bare Minimum Audio

At Bare Minimum Audio, I make your local dive bar show sound like a headline set. You bring the instruments — I bring the gear, the experience, and everything it takes to turn it up to 11.

From small bar stages to outdoor festival rigs, I handle sound and lighting for bands and events of all sizes across the Madison area. No fuss, no drama — just great sound and an engineer who knows what he's doing.
